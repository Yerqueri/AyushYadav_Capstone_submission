02 · Stage 1 Discovery: Interview Discrepancies vs Data

Resolving Perception vs. Empirical Data Conflicts.

Where stakeholder accounts disagreed, and how ticket data audit established objective truth.

The Identified Conflict Stakeholder Perception Claim Empirical Ticket Data Reality Operational Architecture Decision
1. Escalation Cause: Volume vs. Findability Marcus: Escalation is a human staffing capacity problem.
Daniel: ~50% are answerable at L1 with findable docs.		 49.1% of escalations (138/281) were answerable_from_docs=true, plus 92 auto-respond tickets escalated. Daniel was correct! Escalations were artificially inflated by retrieval failure. Build RAG-assisted triage at L1.
2. Non-Fluent Language CSAT Penalty Sofia: Non-fluent English tickets receive the worst satisfaction scores. Non-fluent CSAT is 3.04 vs 2.95 fluent (slightly higher!). The real penalty is +14% handling time (466m vs 408m decoding delay). Target intent extraction to solve agent decoding delays (+14% time), not CSAT-focused routing.
3. Enterprise Service Speed Perception Ravi: Enterprise tier receives faster responses in ~1 hour. Enterprise actually had the worst operational metrics (484m resolution, 37.3% FCR, 62.7% escalation). Standard tier is largest & worst-served. Focus automation throughput on Standard tier (51% volume) while safeguarding Enterprise complexity.
4. The Hidden Channel Opportunity All Stakeholders: Nobody named the docs_comment channel unprompted. Accounts for 15.6% volume. Worst FCR (34.6%) & longest resolution (515m), but highest CSAT (3.17). Prioritize docs_comment for auto-response — highest customer satisfaction ROI.

06 · Safety, Governance & Guardrails

Enterprise Guardrails & Security Pipeline.

Comprehensive multi-layer validation package (`src/guardrails/`) enforcing privacy, prompt security, and auditability.

Ingress Security

1. Ingress Validation

  • Prompt Injection Detector: Intercepts adversarial jailbreaks and system prompt override attempts.
  • Jailbreak Classifier: Scans for malicious instruction hijacking before agent processing.
  • Action: Immediately flags ticket and routes to L2 Human Review queue with safety tag.
Egress Security

2. Egress & Privacy Validation

  • PII Masking Scanner: Scans outbound text for credit cards, SSNs, API tokens, and customer names.
  • Prompt Leakage Prevention: Blocks generated drafts that reveal internal system prompts or rules.
  • Toxic & Harm Filter: Enforces zero-tolerance safety on outbound customer language.
Auditability & Resolution

3. Resolution & Audit Store

  • Zero API Key Dependency: Resolved Guardrails AI API key dependency by integrating open-source PyPI packages natively.
  • SQLite Decision Repository: Persists 100% of guardrail validation check results to storage/decisions.db.
  • Audit Compliance: Ready for autumn compliance review.

07 · Stage 5 PRD Revision Log

Stage 5 PRD Revisions: Hypothesis vs Realized Architecture.

Compulsory post-evaluation revisions logged in 05_PRD_Revision_Log.pdf based on empirical findings.

Requirement ID PRD v1.0 Initial Hypothesis PRD v2.0 Revised Architecture Empirical Trigger & Engineering Rationale
FR-01 (Classification) Train custom supervised ML classifiers (DeBERTa / Scikit-Learn). Use Few-Shot LLM Prompts for V1; set Fine-Tuned ML Models as Phase 2 Northstar. Pre-project baseline was 100% manual human triage with noisy labels. Spreading 500 tickets over 22 intent classes (~22 samples/class) caused supervised ML to overfit. LLM few-shot prompting enforced objective rules in V1.
FR-02 (Escalation) EXIT A early stop (no draft generated on escalations). FLAG A Copilot drafting (RAG pre-drafts attached for human review). EXIT A early aborts forced human agents back into 100% manual drafting (~12 min/ticket). Copilot pre-drafting saved ~17.8 hours of human effort.
FR-04 (Grounding) Strict sentence substring matching. Relaxed sentence substring matching; RAG candidate filtering. Strict sentence matching caused false-positive blocks on valid drafts synthesizing multiple articles. Relaxing increased FCR to 61.0% with 0 false claims.
CSAT Policy Targeted 4.0/5 CSAT upliftment. Categorized in MOSCoW "Won't Have" (CSAT Non-Intervention). CSAT (3.2/5) is an operational symptom of long delays (8-12 hrs) and low FCR (<50%), with an FCR ceiling of 3.41. CSAT cannot be inflated via prompt phrasing alone without fixing response latency and FCR.

10 · First Contact Resolution Framework

Why Policy Suppression does NOT decrease FCR.

Deliberately suppressing direct AI auto-response is a governance safety choice — not a capability limitation.

Upfront AI Reasoning

1. Complete Pre-Computation

The L1 AI Agent parses context, retrieves canonical RAG docs, and pre-drafts the exact resolution before any human opens the ticket.

Single-Touch Approval

2. L2 Human Review (~1-2m)

When the L2 Human Agent opens the ticket, they inspect the ready draft, make any minor edits, and click send on first touch.

Customer Experience

3. First Contact Resolution

The customer receives a fast, doc-grounded answer on the very first human touchpoint with zero multi-tier transfers or back-and-forth loops.
